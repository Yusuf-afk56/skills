About Dr Schirwani

A distinguished academic clinician with extensive NHS and research experience.

Dr Schaida Schirwani is a Consultant Clinical Geneticist at the Wessex Clinical Genetics Service, University Hospital Southampton NHS Foundation Trust. With advanced training in medical genetics and general internal medicine (MRCP), she brings a uniquely broad clinical perspective to every patient encounter.

Her clinical work encompasses the assessment, diagnosis, and management of individuals and families with a wide range of genetic conditions — from complex neurodevelopmental presentations in children to rare dysmorphic syndromes and hereditary cancer predisposition syndromes.

Dr Schirwani is also an active researcher, having published over 32 peer-reviewed articles and contributed to internationally recognised genetic databases including GeneReviews (NIH). She is committed to translating cutting-edge genomic science into meaningful clinical care.

In private practice, Dr Schirwani offers timely access to specialist genetic assessment, detailed counselling, and co-ordination with other specialists — supporting families through what can often be a complex and emotional journey.

Clinical Specialties

Areas of Expertise

Providing specialist assessment and genetic counselling across a broad spectrum of conditions, with particular depth in rare and complex presentations.

Neurodevelopmental Disorders

Specialist assessment of developmental delay, intellectual disability, autism spectrum disorder (ASD), ADHD, and complex behavioural presentations with an underlying genetic aetiology.

Dysmorphology

Expert clinical evaluation of individuals with unusual physical features, structural differences, or congenital anomalies to identify underlying genetic syndromes and guide further investigation.

Rare Genetic Conditions

Diagnosis and management of rare, ultra-rare, and novel genetic conditions — including ASXL3-related (Bainbridge-Ropers) syndrome and other recently characterised genomic disorders.

Genetic Counselling

Compassionate, patient-centred counselling to help individuals and families understand the implications of a genetic diagnosis, recurrence risks, and reproductive options.

Genomic Interpretation

Interpretation of whole-exome and whole-genome sequencing results, chromosomal microarray, and gene panel reports — translating complex genomic data into clear clinical guidance.

Paediatric Genetics

Holistic assessment of children referred with suspected genetic syndromes, including joint clinics with paediatric radiology, developmental paediatrics, and metabolic medicine.

Private Practice

What to Expect

Private consultations provide timely access to specialist genetics expertise with a thorough, unhurried approach tailored to your needs.

01

Initial Consultation

A comprehensive genetic assessment including detailed personal and family history, clinical examination where appropriate, and a thorough review of all previous investigations and records.

02

Diagnostic Testing

Arrangement of targeted or broad-based genetic testing — chromosomal microarray, gene panels, exome sequencing — through accredited UK laboratories with expert interpretation.

03

Results & Counselling

A dedicated results appointment to discuss findings clearly and fully, including implications for the individual, siblings, and other family members, with written summary provided.

04

Specialist Coordination

Liaison with other paediatric and adult specialists, referral to NHS services where appropriate, and co-ordination of a multi-disciplinary care plan when required.

05

Follow-Up & Support

Ongoing follow-up appointments as new genetic information emerges, support for families navigating rare disease networks, and written reports suitable for schools and other services.

06

Second Opinion

Expert independent review of previous genetic assessments, variant reclassifications, and diagnostic uncertainty — providing clarity when families have unresolved questions.

GP or specialist referral welcome. Self-referrals are also accepted for private consultations. Most private health insurers recognise Dr Schirwani as a recognised specialist — please check with your insurer prior to booking. A full written report is provided following every appointment.
